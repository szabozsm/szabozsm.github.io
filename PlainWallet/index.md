# PlainWallet User Guide

Welcome to PlainWallet, a simple Android app that keeps all your shopping, loyalty, and membership cards in one place. Open the app, pick a card, and show the barcode at the checkout.

PlainWallet works fully on your device. You do not need an account, and cloud features are optional.

## Where to start

New to PlainWallet? Begin with [Getting Started](getting-started.md). You will have your first card saved in about a minute.

## Finding your way around the app

Tap the menu button (☰) in the top corner to open the menu:

| Menu item | What it does |
|-|-|
| **Home** | Returns to your card list. |
| **Export** | Saves all your cards to a backup file. See [Backup and Restore](backup-and-restore.md). |
| **Import** | Loads cards from a backup file. |
| **Settings** | Changes the card list layout and sets up cloud sync. See [Settings](settings.md). |

At the bottom of the menu you can see the installed app version. Tapping the image there opens the developer's Buy Me a Coffee page, if you would like to support the project.

## Good to know

- Your cards are stored on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data can remove them, so keep a [backup](backup-and-restore.md) of cards you care about.
- Camera access is only needed when you scan a barcode.
- PlainWallet currently runs on Android.
