# Logos and Colors

A logo and a background color make each card quick to spot in your list. Both are optional. Without a logo, PlainWallet shows initials based on the card name.

## Choose a logo

1. Open the card editor (add a new card, or tap **Edit** on an existing one).
2. Tap **Select Logo**.
3. Pick one of the three tabs described below.
4. Back in the editor, tap **Apply** to save the card.

### Built-in tab

Browse the logos that come with PlainWallet.

- Type part of a name in **Filter by filename** to narrow the list.
- Tap a logo to select it.
- Some built-in logos also suggest a matching background color.

### Web tab

Use an image from the internet.

1. Enter the image address (a URL starting with `http` or `https`).
2. Wait for the preview to appear.
3. Tap **Use URL**.

Your device needs an internet connection to load the image. If the website later moves or removes the picture, the logo may stop working.

### File tab

Use an image stored on your device.

1. Tap **Browse for image**.
2. Select the image file.

## Remove a logo

On the **File** tab, tap **Delete logo**. PlainWallet goes back to showing initials.

## Change the background color

In the card editor you can change the card's background color. Pick a color that contrasts well with your logo, so the card is easy to read.

The color picker has two parts.

### Color spectrum

The large panel at the top shows the full range of colors.

- Move left or right to choose a hue, such as red, green, or blue.
- Move up for a lighter shade, fading towards white.
- Move down for a darker shade, fading towards black.

Use the spectrum when you need a specific color, for example to match a store's brand.

### Color swatches

Below the spectrum is a grid of ready-made color swatches, from neutral grays and white through reds, oranges, yellows, greens, blues, and purples, to a few browns. Tap a swatch to use that color. This is the fastest way to get a good result.

> **Tip:** A light card background works best with a dark logo, and a dark background works best with a light logo. Some built-in logos suggest a matching color for you.

## Good to know

- Logos and notes are part of your card data. They are included in [backups](backup-and-restore.md) and [cloud sync](cloud-sync.md).
- If the web logo address cannot be reached, check your internet connection and that the link is correct.
