# Barcodes

PlainWallet turns your card number into a barcode that store scanners can read. This page covers scanning, choosing a format, and resizing.

## How the barcode is made

The barcode on a card's detail screen is generated from two things you provide:

- The **Card number**
- The **Barcode type** (also called the barcode format)

If either is wrong, the barcode may not display or may not scan.

## Scan a barcode

Scanning fills in the number and the barcode type for you, so it is the best choice when you have the physical card.

1. In the card editor, tap the scan icon next to the **Card number** field.
2. If Android asks, allow PlainWallet to use the camera.
3. Hold the barcode in view of the camera until it is recognized.
4. PlainWallet returns to the editor with the number and type filled in.

While scanning, you can use:

- The **camera switch** button to change between cameras.
- The **flashlight** button to light up the barcode in dim places.

## Type a number yourself

You can also type the **Card number** and pick the **Barcode type** from the list.

Some barcode types only accept certain values, for example only digits or a fixed length. If your barcode does not appear, check that:

- The number was typed exactly as printed on your card.
- The selected barcode type matches your card.

If you are unsure of the type, delete the number and scan the card instead.

## Resize the barcode

Open a card and drag the slider below the barcode to make it wider or narrower on screen. Use this to find the size your store's scanner reads most easily. Turning your screen brightness up also helps.
