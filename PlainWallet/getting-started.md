# Getting Started

This page walks you through saving your first card and showing it at the checkout.

## Add your first card

1. Open PlainWallet and tap the **+** button on the Home screen.
2. Type a **Name** for the card, for example the store name. This is the label shown in your card list.
3. Fill in the **Card number**. You can type it, or tap the scan icon next to the field and scan the barcode on your card with the camera.
4. If you typed the number yourself, choose the matching **Barcode type**. If you scanned it, PlainWallet fills in the type for you.
5. Optional: choose a logo, change the card color, or add a note.
6. Tap **Apply** in the top toolbar to save.

Your new card now appears in the list on the Home screen.

> **Tip:** If you are not sure which barcode type your card uses, scanning is the easiest way. PlainWallet detects the type automatically.

## Show a card at the checkout

1. On the Home screen, tap the card.
2. The barcode appears on screen, along with the card name, number, and any notes.
3. Hold your phone up to the scanner.

If the barcode is too small or too large for the scanner, use the slider under the barcode to adjust its width. See [Barcodes](barcodes.md) for more.
