# Managing Your Cards

This page explains how to find, add, view, edit, and delete cards.

## Your card list

The Home screen shows all your cards in alphabetical order by name.

### Search your cards

Type in the **Filter cards...** box at the top of the list. PlainWallet matches your text against each card's name, number, and notes. The search is not case-sensitive, so "coffee" and "Coffee" give the same results.

To see the full list again, clear the box.

> **Note:** The search looks at text only. It cannot search the contents of logo images.

### Switch between one and two columns

1. Open the menu (☰) and tap **Settings**.
2. Under **Card list view**, select the one-column or two-column preview.
3. Tap **Apply**.

## Add a card

1. Tap **+** on the Home screen.
2. Enter a **Name**.
3. Enter the **Card number**, or scan it. See [Barcodes](barcodes.md#scan-a-barcode).
4. Choose the **Barcode type** if you typed the number.
5. Optional: select a logo, change the background color, or add notes. See [Logos and Colors](logos-and-colors.md).
6. Tap **Apply** to save.

If a card has no logo, PlainWallet shows initials made from the card name, so it is still easy to recognize.

## View a card

Tap any card in the list. The detail screen shows:

- The card name
- The card number
- The barcode
- Your notes

Use the slider below the barcode to make it wider or narrower. See [Barcodes](barcodes.md#resize-the-barcode).

## Edit a card

1. Open the card.
2. Tap **Edit** in the top toolbar.
3. Make your changes.
4. Tap **Apply** to save.

To leave the editor without saving, use your device's Back action.

## Delete a card

1. Open the card and tap **Edit**.
2. Tap **Delete**.
3. Confirm when asked.

The card is removed from your device. If cloud sync is turned on, the deletion is also sent to your cloud provider so your other devices follow. See [Cloud Sync](cloud-sync.md).

> **Caution:** Deleting a card cannot be undone from within the app. If you might want it back, make a [backup](backup-and-restore.md) first.

## Where your cards are stored

Cards live on your device. Uninstalling PlainWallet or clearing its app data can remove them. To keep a second copy, make a backup file or set up cloud sync.
